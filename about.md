## About Me


I am Guillermo R.S., a software engineer with more than twenty years of experience who is undergoing a structured transition into cybersecurity. My current focus is on defensive security, technical vulnerability analysis, DevSecOps, and process‑oriented professional documentation.


My way of working is based on principles that have guided my entire career and that now form the core of my security profile:

- **clear methodology**, to approach complex problems with structure and sound criteria 

- **reproducible analysis**, avoiding improvisation and ensuring traceability  

- **structured documentation**, enabling clear understanding of processes, decisions, and results 

- **continuous improvement**, consistently incorporating new practices and tools

- **practical, hands‑on learning**, centered on real projects and controlled environments


My transition into cybersecurity is not an improvised change but a planned process. I designed an **intensive, modular plan** covering network auditing, vulnerability analysis, hardening, scripting, SOC workflows, DevSecOps, and the creation of a complete technical portfolio. This approach allows me to demonstrate real capabilities, not just theoretical knowledge.


Before starting this transition, I developed my career in complex enterprise environments, working both with legacy systems and modern solutions built from scratch. I have participated in migrations, distributed architectures, APIs, databases, and cloud platforms, always with a methodical and quality‑oriented approach. This combination of experience — maintaining, modernizing, and creating — is the foundation on which I am building my security profile.
